# Cat Care Guide

## Feeding

Adult cats are usually fed two measured meals a day, though some owners split the
same amount into three or four smaller portions. Kittens under six months need to
eat more often, typically three or four times a day, to support fast growth. Cats
should always have access to fresh water, and many prefer a water fountain over a
still bowl. Feeding is a high priority task, and it matters even more when a cat is
on medication that must be given with food.

## Play and enrichment

Cats are natural hunters and need daily play to stay healthy and avoid boredom.
Two short sessions of 10 to 15 minutes with a wand toy or laser, plus climbing
spaces and scratching posts, keep an indoor cat stimulated. Enrichment is a medium
priority task: it prevents behavior problems like scratching furniture, but it can
be shortened on a busy day.

## Litter box

A cat's litter box should be scooped at least once a day and fully cleaned weekly.
Cats are picky about cleanliness, and a dirty box is a leading reason cats stop
using it. Scooping is a short but high priority task because problems here are hard
to reverse.

## Grooming

Short-haired cats groom themselves and only need occasional brushing, while
long-haired breeds such as Persians need daily brushing to prevent painful mats.
Nail trims every couple of weeks help protect furniture and people. Grooming is
usually a low priority task unless the cat is long-haired.
