# Dog Care Guide

## Exercise and walks

Most adult dogs need at least 30 to 60 minutes of physical activity every day,
usually split across one or two walks. High-energy breeds such as Border Collies,
Huskies, and Shibas often need more, closer to 60 to 90 minutes, or they may become
restless and destructive. Puppies should get shorter, more frequent walks: a common
rule of thumb is about five minutes of walking per month of age, up to twice a day.
Skipping exercise is one of the most common causes of behavior problems, so a daily
walk is usually a high priority task.

## Feeding

Adult dogs are typically fed twice a day, in the morning and the evening. Puppies
under six months usually need three or four smaller meals a day because they cannot
eat enough in one sitting. Fresh water should always be available. Feeding is a
high priority task because a missed meal affects the dog's energy and mood, and
because medications are often given with food.

## Enrichment and play

Dogs need mental stimulation as well as physical exercise. Fifteen to thirty minutes
of play, training, or puzzle toys each day helps prevent boredom. Enrichment is
usually a medium priority: important for a happy dog, but it can be shortened on a
busy day once the walk and feeding are done.

## Grooming

Most dogs need brushing a few times a week, and long-haired breeds may need it daily
to prevent matting. Nail trims are needed roughly every three to four weeks. Grooming
is generally a low to medium priority task that can be scheduled when time allows.
