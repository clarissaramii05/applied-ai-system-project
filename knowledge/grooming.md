# Grooming

## Brushing

Brushing removes loose fur, spreads natural oils, and prevents painful mats.
Short-haired pets usually need brushing once or twice a week, while long-haired
dogs and cats often need it daily. Regular brushing also gives the owner a chance
to notice lumps, ticks, or skin problems early. Brushing is normally a low to
medium priority task that fits well into leftover time.

## Nail trims

Nails that grow too long can crack, catch, or change how an animal walks. Most dogs
and cats need a nail trim every three to four weeks. If nails click loudly on hard
floors, they are due for a trim. Owners who are nervous about cutting too far can
ask a vet or groomer to demonstrate.

## Bathing

Dogs generally need a bath only every one to three months unless they get dirty,
because bathing too often strips protective oils and dries the skin. Most cats
rarely need baths at all and clean themselves. Bathing is a low priority, occasional
task rather than a daily one.

## Teeth and ears

Dental care is often overlooked. Brushing a pet's teeth several times a week, or
using vet-approved dental chews, helps prevent painful disease. Ears should be
checked regularly and gently cleaned if they look dirty or smell, especially in
floppy-eared breeds.
