# Feeding and Nutrition

## How often to feed

Most adult dogs and cats do well on two meals a day, spaced roughly morning and
evening. Young puppies and kittens need more frequent meals, usually three or four
times a day, because their small stomachs cannot hold a full day of food at once.
Older pets can usually stay on two meals a day unless a vet advises otherwise.

## Portion sizes

Portion size depends on the animal's weight, age, and activity level. The feeding
guide on the food package is a starting point, not a rule, and many pets are
overfed. If a pet is gaining or losing weight unexpectedly, the portion should be
reviewed with a vet rather than guessed at.

## Water

Fresh, clean water should be available at all times for both dogs and cats.
Refilling and cleaning the water bowl daily is a quick but important task, because
dehydration is dangerous and develops faster than many owners expect.

## Treats and medication

Treats should make up no more than about ten percent of daily calories. Many
medications are given with food to reduce stomach upset, which is one reason a
regular feeding schedule matters: a missed meal can mean a missed dose.
